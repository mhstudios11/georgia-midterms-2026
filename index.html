Top of the ticket

Georgia's two biggest races. Georgia's U.S. senators are 2 of the 100 votes on every federal law, and they confirm Supreme Court justices and cabinet members; each serves a six-year term. The governor runs the state for four years, proposing Georgia's budget and signing or vetoing every bill the legislature passes.

Statewide Offices

The officials who run Georgia day to day, from elections and public schools to the rates on your power bill.

Most serve four-year terms; Public Service Commissioners serve six.

U.S. House

Georgia sends 14 representatives to the U.S. House, the half of Congress that writes the federal budget and starts every tax bill. Each member serves a two-year term, so all 14 seats are on the ballot every even year. Together they're 14 of the 435 votes that decide which party controls the House.
